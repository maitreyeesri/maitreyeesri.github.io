About

Engineer by training.Product by choice.

Mphoto coming soon

I'm Maitreyee, a product manager who likes the unglamorous middle of a problem: after the pitch, before the launch, where you find out what people actually need.

I'm the first product hire at WeDoGood, an NGO-sector organisation building products for under-served communities. I lead two products from zero: ThinkWith, AI-led career mentoring for young people over WhatsApp, and Givo, a donation and compliance platform for NGOs.

Before that I spent nearly three years at HireQuotient, first on product and GTM, then owning the core AI hiring product, EasySource. And before that, I wrote security systems at Standard Chartered.

Off the clock you'll find me reading, painting or writing.

I studied aerospace engineering at IIT Kanpur, ran the consulting club there, and volunteered with the campus Covid support effort in 2021. Somewhere in there I learned I like building for people more than for planes.

More of that side lives on the off hours page.
